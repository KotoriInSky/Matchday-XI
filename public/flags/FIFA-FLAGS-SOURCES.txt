MEMBERSHIP AND SEARCH DATA / 会籍与搜索数据
The 211 association names and FIFA codes were checked against:
https://inside.fifa.com/associations
The catalog is maintained by Matchday XI; FIFA's bitmap artwork is not bundled.
Local names use Intl.DisplayNames with football-association overrides.
Current IOC codes were cross-checked against the current NOC table at:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_IOC_country_codes
Only factual identifiers are included, not article prose or illustrations.
IOC identifiers are search aliases. GBR finds England, Scotland, Wales and
Northern Ireland; this does not add an IOC-only entry to the FIFA catalog.
LIB and SIN are also accepted as former codes for Lebanon and Singapore.

211 个名称和 FIFA 代码按 FIFA 官方会员协会名录核对。
IOC 代码只是搜索别名；GBR 对应四个英国足协会籍，不增加会籍条目。
该目录按体育协会会籍编排，不作为政治实体或主权分类。
项目与 FIFA、IOC 或各协会没有官方隶属、赞助或认可关系。

project JSON as image data. Search makes no requests to FIFA, IOC or Wikimedia.
